ABOUT SAILORCAREER

Building a smarter future for maritime careers.

SailorCareer is a dedicated maritime career and recruitment platform designed to make the journey from training to employment more structured, transparent and professional for seafarers and employers.

Explore Jobs →Create Seafarer Profile
WHO WE ARE

One platform for the people who keep the maritime industry moving.

SailorCareer brings together seafarers, maritime career resources and employers in one focused digital environment. Our goal is to reduce confusion around career progression and help candidates present their professional experience in a clear, structured way.

For employers, the platform is designed to support organized recruitment and candidate discovery. For seafarers, it provides tools and information that can help them prepare, build their profile and find relevant opportunities.

OUR MISSION

Make maritime career opportunities easier to discover, understand and pursue.

01

For Seafarers

Help seafarers build professional profiles, organize career information, discover vacancies and understand the steps needed to progress in the maritime industry.

02

For Employers

Provide a structured environment for employers to publish vacancies, review suitable candidate profiles and manage recruitment workflows.

03

For the Industry

Encourage better organized maritime recruitment through clearer information, professional profiles and responsible access to candidate data.

OUR VISION

To become a trusted digital destination for maritime careers and recruitment.

We envision a maritime ecosystem where a seafarer can understand the career path ahead, maintain a strong professional profile and discover opportunities without unnecessary complexity — while employers can access relevant talent through a more organized recruitment process.

WHAT WE OFFER

Designed around real maritime career needs.

⚓ Seafarer Profiles

Structured professional information for rank, experience, certificates, preferences and career details.

🚢 Maritime Jobs

Job discovery focused on ranks, vessel types, sectors, locations and joining requirements.

📄 Professional CV

Tools intended to help eligible seafarers present their maritime experience in a professional format.

📈 Career Support

Guidance covering career planning, watchkeeping, CoC, DC/CDC and fresher-oriented maritime information.

🔔 Job Alerts

Relevant alerts and career updates to help users stay aware of opportunities.

🏢 Employer Recruitment

A dedicated employer environment for verified recruitment activities and vacancy management.

OUR APPROACH

Professional first

Maritime careers depend on accurate professional information. We aim to keep profiles structured and useful for recruitment.

Privacy-conscious

Candidate information should be accessed responsibly. Platform features are designed around account access, permissions and controlled recruitment use.

Career-focused

We focus on practical information that helps seafarers move from preparation to application and career progression.

Industry-specific

SailorCareer is built specifically around seafarers, maritime ranks, vessels, certificates, experience and recruitment needs.

OUR COMMITMENT

Better information. Better preparation. Better connections.

SailorCareer is committed to developing a platform that is useful to both sides of the maritime recruitment ecosystem. We will continue improving career tools, vacancy discovery, profile capabilities and employer workflows as the platform grows.

No placement fee is charged to seafarers.
BUILDING A MARITIME COMMUNITY

Building a professional maritime community.

SailorCareer is not intended to be just another job listing website.

Our long-term objective is to build a professional maritime community where career information, recruitment opportunities and professional profiles come together.

From a fresher beginning their maritime career to an experienced officer looking for their next command or assignment, SailorCareer aims to support the career journey at every stage.
