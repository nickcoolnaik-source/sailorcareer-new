What you'll explore

Practical knowledge for your next career step

Sessions combine operational insight, career planning and participant questions. Topics are educational and do not replace official regulatory guidance.

01 / CAREER PATHWAYS

Cadet sponsorship & entry routes

Eligibility, application preparation, interviews, documents, offer evaluation and how to identify misleading recruitment claims.

02 / SHIPBOARD LEADERSHIP

Master Mariner perspective

Bridge watchkeeping, navigation, BRM, ISM/SMS, emergency readiness, crew leadership and Deck progression.

03 / RECRUITMENT

Shipowner & crewing insights

Screening, CVs, interviews, safe manning, SEA, MLC awareness, joining documentation and fraud prevention.

04 / ENGINE CAREERS

Chief Engineer perspective

Engine hierarchy, watchkeeping, PMS, ERM, permits, risk assessment, MARPOL, troubleshooting and maintenance records.

05 / TRAINING & CERTIFICATION

Training pathways explained

DNS, BSc Nautical Science, Marine Engineering, GP Rating, STCW, institute verification, CoC/CoP, CDC and INDOS.

06 / CAREER DEVELOPMENT

Promotion readiness

Rank competencies, sea-service records, certification milestones, communication, assessments and an individual development plan.

No. It is an educational masterclass. It does not guarantee employment, cadet sponsorship, vessel joining or promotion.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible participants who complete both days will receive a SailorCareer Certificate of Participation. It is not a statutory maritime qualification or DG Shipping certificate.

Are the dates and times confirmed?

The listed weekend options are 17–18 October 2026 and 24–25 October 2026, 10 AM–6 PM IST each day. Joining and delivery details will be shared with confirmed participants.
